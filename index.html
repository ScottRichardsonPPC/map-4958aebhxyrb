    Tracks come from Scott's GPS logs and are simplified, so times, distances and heights are approximate. Height is above the ground where he took off, not exactly above your spot. Some flights were never GPS-tracked.

    Purple dots are photos Scott posted on Facebook, placed by the camera's GPS or the location stamped on the photo. Links open the full Facebook post (all photos and the caption); Facebook may ask you to log in. Gold dots (turn on “Best-of photos (4–5★)” at top right) are Scott’s 4- and 5-star photos that were also posted on Facebook, placed where the aircraft was when each was taken; the subjects listed with them are guesses.
